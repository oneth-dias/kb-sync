Release notes

KB Sync updates automatically; there's nothing to install.

November 2026: KB Sync for Freshdesk

  • A separate app that publishes Confluence page trees to a Freshdesk knowledge base: categories, folders and subfolders, articles, images and styled panels. See KB Sync for Freshdesk.
  • Both apps: the edit panel on the settings page now shows a tree's current layout and publishing choices.

November 2026: first release

  • Publish a Confluence page or folder, and everything under it, to a Zendesk help center category.
  • Mirror the page tree as sections (nested on Guide Enterprise, path-named on other plans), or use one section.
  • Edits, new pages, renames and moves reach Zendesk within seconds, with a full check every hour.
  • Images and attached files are uploaded; links between synced pages point to the matching articles.
  • Panels and status labels keep their styling in any help center theme.
  • Drafts or published articles, user-segment visibility, multiple brands, and the help center's default language.
  • Deleted pages are archived in Zendesk, never deleted.
  • Zendesk OAuth sign-in; KB Sync stores no personal data.
  • Settings page: setup help and status for every synced tree.
