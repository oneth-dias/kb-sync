Troubleshooting

The message you see on KB Sync's settings page, and what to do about it.

Connecting Zendesk

You seeDo this
"Enter your Zendesk subdomain"Enter only the part before .zendesk.com: acme, not https://acme.zendesk.com. If you use a custom help center domain, still enter the zendesk.com subdomain.
"Zendesk did not authorize KB Sync"Click Connect Zendesk again and choose Allow. Sign in as a Zendesk admin or help center manager; agents without help center rights can't approve it.
"This sign-in link has expired or was already used"Sign-in links last 15 minutes and work once. Start again from the settings page.
The settings page doesn't show Connected after you allowed accessReload the settings page. If it still isn't connected, check that you approved the same subdomain you entered.
"Zendesk sign-in has expired or was revoked"Someone removed KB Sync's access in Zendesk, or it expired. Click Disconnect, then Connect Zendesk again. Existing articles are matched up again, not duplicated.

Adding a page tree

You seeDo this
"Paste a Confluence page or folder link"Copy the link from the browser's address bar while viewing the page, or use Share > Copy link.
"KB Sync can't find that page or folder"Check the link is from this Confluence site. Pages with view restrictions may not be readable by KB Sync; remove the restriction or choose another page.
"… is already inside …, which is being synced"That page is already part of another synced tree. Each page can only be synced once.
"You can sync up to 25 page trees"Remove a tree you no longer need, or sync a parent page that covers several trees.

Syncing

You seeDo this
Status stays at SyncingLarge trees sync in batches of about 12 minutes. A 1,000-page first sync can take up to half an hour. Leave it running; later syncs only send changes.
Status shows FailedThe status shows the reason. Fix it, then click Sync now. Failed trees are retried by the hourly check, and nothing synced before the failure is duplicated.
"Only the first 10,000 pages are synced"Only the first 10,000 pages are synced. Split the tree into smaller trees.
An edit hasn't reached ZendeskCheck the page is published, not a draft. Then click Sync now, or wait for the hourly check.

In Zendesk

You seeDo this
Articles don't appear in the help centerThey're probably drafts (the default). Publish them in Zendesk, or Edit the tree and choose Publish straight away. Also check the visibility setting: articles for a user segment only show to signed-in members of it.
Sections are side by side, named like Admin › BillingYour Zendesk plan doesn't include sections inside sections (Guide Enterprise). This is expected; the path keeps the structure readable.
An image is missingCheck the image is attached to a page KB Sync can read. Images that can't be copied are left out rather than shown broken.
A link opens Confluence instead of an articleThe link points to a page outside the synced tree. Add that page to a synced tree, or link to something public.
Formatting looks differentYour help center theme styles articles. Callouts and status labels carry their own styling; everything else follows your theme.
My edits in Zendesk disappearedSynced articles are overwritten when their Confluence page changes. Make content changes in Confluence.

Other

You seeDo this
"KB Sync's license isn't active"A site admin can subscribe, or restart the subscription, in Atlassian Administration > Apps.
You can't find the settings pageOnly site admins can open it. See Open KB Sync's settings.
