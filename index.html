KB Sync for Zendesk

Write your help articles in Confluence. KB Sync publishes them to your Zendesk help center and keeps them up to date. Every edit reaches Zendesk within seconds.

Set up (5 minutes)

You need to be a Confluence admin, and able to sign in to Zendesk as an admin or a help center manager.

  1. Install KB Sync from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Open KB Sync's settings page: go to admin.atlassian.com > Apps > Sites > your site > Connected apps. Find KB Sync, click ⋯, then Configure. Bookmark the page that opens; that's where you'll manage the app.
  3. Enter your Zendesk subdomain (the acme in acme.zendesk.com) and click Connect Zendesk. Sign in and click Allow on the Zendesk tab. The settings page shows Connected when you're done.
  4. Under Add a page tree, paste a link to the Confluence page or folder that holds your help content. Then choose a Zendesk category, a layout, and whether articles start as drafts. Click Add and sync.

Step-by-step details, with every option explained, are in Installation and setup.

The first sync of a few hundred pages takes a few minutes. After that, each edit you publish in Confluence reaches Zendesk within seconds.

How syncing works

  • What's synced: the page or folder you choose, and every published page under it. Each page becomes one Zendesk article.
  • When: within seconds when a page is published, edited or moved. Deleted pages are picked up by the hourly check, because Confluence doesn't notify apps about deletions.
  • One direction: Confluence is the source of truth. Edits made to a synced article in Zendesk are overwritten the next time its page changes. Article settings that KB Sync doesn't manage, such as labels, comments, votes and subscriptions, are left alone.
  • Deleted or moved out: if a page is deleted, or moved outside the synced tree, its article is archived in Zendesk, not deleted. You can restore it from the Zendesk archive.
  • Restored pages: Confluence restores a page from the trash to the top of its space. Move it back into the synced tree, and KB Sync publishes it again as a new article.
  • Stop syncing: removing a page tree, or disconnecting Zendesk, stops syncing. Articles already in Zendesk stay as they are.

Layout in Zendesk

OptionWhat you get
Mirror the page tree (default)Each Confluence folder, and each page with children, becomes a Zendesk section. A parent page becomes the first article in its own section. Pages directly under the top page or folder go into a section named after it. Article order follows the page order in Confluence.
One sectionAll articles go into a single section named after the top page or folder.

Sections inside sections need a Zendesk plan that includes them (Guide Enterprise). On other plans, KB Sync creates sections side by side and names them by their path, for example Admin › Billing.

Macros that only make sense inside Confluence, such as search boxes, "create page" buttons, reports and recently-updated lists, are left out. Most third-party formatting macros keep their content but lose their styling. In our test of 1,698 real help-center pages, about 97% converted usefully.
