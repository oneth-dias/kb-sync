Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers KB Sync for Zendesk ("KB Sync", "the app"), a Confluence Cloud app made by F & D Consulting ("we", "us"), Sri Lanka. Questions: oneth@workwithfd.com.

Summary

  • KB Sync runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We don't operate servers that receive your data.
  • The app reads the Confluence pages you choose and writes them to the Zendesk account you connect. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

What the app processes, and why

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
Content of the Confluence pages and folders you choose to sync: titles, body and attached imagesTo publish them as Zendesk articlesNot stored by the app. It's read from Confluence and written to your Zendesk account.
Sync records: Confluence page IDs and version numbers, page titles, Zendesk article, section and category IDs and URLsTo update the right article, and to skip unchanged pagesForge app storage on your Confluence site
Settings: the page trees you chose, the Zendesk category, the layout and publishing options, and your Zendesk subdomainTo run the sync as configuredForge app storage
Zendesk OAuth access and refresh tokensTo call the Zendesk Help Center API on your behalfForge encrypted secret storage
Email address or name of the Zendesk user who connected the appShown on the settings page, so admins know which account is connectedForge app storage

Forge app storage belongs to your Confluence site's installation of the app. It is hosted by Atlassian, follows your site's data residency settings, and isn't shared between customers.

Who can access it

  • You: Confluence admins can see the settings and sync status. Content in Zendesk is controlled by your Zendesk permissions.
  • Us: we can't read your Confluence content, your Zendesk content or your app storage. Atlassian gives us operational logs. The app is built to log only Confluence page IDs, Zendesk article IDs, counts and error messages, not page content. They're kept for Atlassian's standard log retention period. We use them only to give support and fix problems.
  • Service providers: Atlassian (hosting, through Forge) and Zendesk (the account you connect). Each processes data under its own terms with you.

Scopes

The app asks for Confluence permissions to read pages, folders, attachments and the page hierarchy, plus its own storage. It asks for read and write access to the Zendesk Help Center only (hc:read hc:write). It doesn't ask for, and can't use, permission to change Confluence content or to see Zendesk tickets or users.

Retention and deletion

  • Removing a page tree: deletes its sync records from app storage.
  • Disconnecting Zendesk: deletes the stored tokens and revokes the access token in Zendesk.
  • Uninstalling the app: Atlassian deletes the app's storage for your site under its Forge data-retention policy.
  • Articles in Zendesk: they're your content in your Zendesk account. Removing or uninstalling the app doesn't delete them.

Personal data

Where page content or the connected user's email contains personal data, we act as a processor for you, the customer. We process it only to provide the app, as set out above. To exercise data-protection rights, or to ask for a data processing agreement, email oneth@workwithfd.com.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Tokens are kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage, and the app asks only for the permissions listed above. To report a security issue, email oneth@workwithfd.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the date above and announce the change in the app's Marketplace listing.
