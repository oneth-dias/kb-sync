Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers KB Sync for Zendesk and KB Sync for Freshdesk (each "KB Sync" or "the app"), Confluence Cloud apps made by F & D Consulting ("we", "us"), Sri Lanka. Questions: oneth@workwithfd.com.

Summary

  • KB Sync runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We don't operate servers that receive your data.
  • The app reads the Confluence pages you choose and writes them to the Zendesk or Freshdesk account you connect. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

What the app processes, and why

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
Content of the Confluence pages and folders you choose to sync: titles, body and attached imagesTo publish them as help center articlesNot stored by the app. It's read from Confluence and written to your Zendesk or Freshdesk account.
Sync records: Confluence page IDs and version numbers, page titles, Zendesk or Freshdesk article, section or folder, and category IDs and URLsTo update the right article, and to skip unchanged pagesForge app storage on your Confluence site
Settings: the page trees you chose, the category, the layout, publishing and visibility options, and your Zendesk subdomain or Freshdesk addressTo run the sync as configuredForge app storage
Zendesk OAuth access and refresh tokens (KB Sync for Zendesk)To call the Zendesk Help Center API on your behalfForge encrypted secret storage
Freshdesk API key (KB Sync for Freshdesk)To call the Freshdesk knowledge base API on your behalfForge encrypted secret storage. It's never shown again, sent back to the browser, or written to logs.

Forge app storage belongs to your Confluence site's installation of the app. It is hosted by Atlassian, follows your site's data residency settings, and isn't shared between customers.

Who can access it

  • You: Confluence admins can see the settings and sync status. Content in Zendesk or Freshdesk is controlled by that product's permissions.
  • Us: we can't read your Confluence content, your Zendesk content or your app storage. Atlassian gives us operational logs. The app is built to log only Confluence page IDs, Zendesk article IDs, counts and error messages, not page content or keys. They're kept for Atlassian's standard log retention period. We use them only to give support and fix problems.
  • Service providers: Atlassian (hosting, through Forge) and Zendesk or Freshdesk (the account you connect). Each processes data under its own terms with you.

Scopes

The app asks for Confluence permissions to read pages, folders, attachments and the page hierarchy, plus its own storage. KB Sync for Zendesk asks for read and write access to the Zendesk Help Center only (hc:read hc:write), and can't see Zendesk tickets or users. KB Sync for Freshdesk uses the API key you provide, which carries the permissions of the agent it belongs to; the app only calls Freshdesk's knowledge base (solutions) API. Neither app can change Confluence content.

Retention and deletion

  • Removing a page tree: deletes its sync records from app storage.
  • Disconnecting Zendesk: deletes the stored tokens and revokes the access token in Zendesk.
  • Disconnecting Freshdesk: deletes the stored API key. You can also reset the key in Freshdesk (Profile settings) at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app: Atlassian deletes the app's storage for your site under its Forge data-retention policy.
  • Published articles: they're your content in your Zendesk or Freshdesk account. Removing or uninstalling the app doesn't delete them.

Personal data

The app doesn't store personal data. Where page content passing through it contains personal data, we act as a processor for you, the customer. We process it only to provide the app, as set out above. To exercise data-protection rights, or to ask for a data processing agreement, email oneth@workwithfd.com.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Tokens and API keys are kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage, and the app asks only for the permissions listed above. To report a security issue, email oneth@workwithfd.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the date above and announce the change in the app's Marketplace listing.
