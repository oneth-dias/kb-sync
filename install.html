Installation and setup

From install to your first synced articles in about five minutes.

Before you start

You needWhy
Confluence Cloud, and site admin rightsOnly site admins can install apps and open KB Sync's settings page.
A Zendesk account with a help center (Guide)Articles are published to your help center.
A Zendesk admin or help center manager sign-inYou sign in once to let KB Sync manage help center content.
Optional: Guide EnterpriseOnly needed for sections inside sections. On other plans KB Sync names sections by their path instead, for example Admin › Billing.

1. Install the app

  1. Open KB Sync's page on the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Click Try it free (or Get it now), and choose your Confluence site.
  3. Review the permissions (listed below) and click Install.

Sites with up to 10 Confluence users can use KB Sync for free. Larger sites start with Atlassian's free trial, then are billed per user through Atlassian.

2. Open KB Sync's settings

  1. Go to admin.atlassian.com > Apps > Sites > your site > Connected apps.
  2. Find KB Sync, click ⋯, then Configure.

Bookmark the page that opens. It's where you manage everything: the Zendesk connection, the page trees you sync, and their status.

3. Connect Zendesk

  1. Under Zendesk connection, enter your Zendesk subdomain: the acme in acme.zendesk.com.
  2. Click Connect Zendesk. A Zendesk tab opens.
  3. Sign in as a Zendesk admin or help center manager, and click Allow.
  4. Close that tab. The settings page shows Connected by itself within a few seconds.

KB Sync asks Zendesk only for help center access (hc:read, hc:write). It can't see tickets, users or any other Zendesk data.

4. Add a page tree

  1. Under Add a page tree, paste a link to the Confluence page or folder that holds your help content. Short links (/wiki/x/…) work too.
  2. Choose the options below, then click Add and sync.
OptionChoices
Zendesk brandLeave empty for your main help center. For another brand, enter its subdomain and click Use this brand.
Zendesk categoryA new category named after the page (default), or one of your existing categories.
LayoutMirror the page tree: folders and pages with children become sections. One section: every article in a single section.
New articlesDrafts (default), so you can review them first, or Publish straight away.
Who can see the articlesEveryone, or one of your Zendesk user segments.

You can change the layout, publishing and visibility later with Edit. The category and brand are fixed once a tree is added; to change them, remove the tree and add it again.

5. Check the result in Zendesk

  • The page tree's status shows Syncing, then Synced with the number of articles. Click the category name to open it in your help center.
  • If you chose drafts, find them in Zendesk under Knowledge admin > Manage articles > Drafts, and publish them when you're happy.
  • From now on, each edit published in Confluence reaches Zendesk within seconds. Everything is also checked once an hour.

What KB Sync can access

WhereAccessUsed for
ConfluenceRead pages, folders, attachments and page hierarchyReading the content you choose to sync. KB Sync never changes Confluence content.
ConfluenceApp storageSettings, and which page matches which article.
ZendeskHelp center read and writeCreating and updating categories, sections, articles and article images.

KB Sync runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. Page content goes from Confluence straight to your Zendesk account. See the privacy policy.

Stop syncing or uninstall

  • Stop one tree: click Remove next to it. Its articles stay in Zendesk.
  • Disconnect Zendesk: click Disconnect. KB Sync revokes its Zendesk sign-in, and syncing stops. Articles stay in Zendesk.
  • Uninstall: click Disconnect first, then go to admin.atlassian.com > Apps > Sites > your site > Connected apps > ⋯ > Remove app. Atlassian deletes KB Sync's stored settings. Articles already in Zendesk are yours and stay where they are.

Updates are installed automatically by Atlassian; there's nothing to upgrade by hand.
